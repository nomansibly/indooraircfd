03 / Reproducible by design

From geometry to evidence.

Full-depth slots in an extruded two-dimensional section. No furniture or resolved occupants.

01 · Conservative airflow and scalar transport

Sparse finite-volume potential-flow solution with prescribed, balanced vent fluxes. First-order upwind advection and centered diffusion transport temperature and local mean air age. Effective scalar diffusivity is assumed at 0.01 m²/s.

02 · Fanger thermal comfort

Local PMV and PPD use 50% relative humidity, 1.2 met, 0.5 clo, and 26°C mean radiant temperature. Comfort-model air speed is floored at 0.05 m/s. These are assumed inputs, not measurements.

03 · Verification and mesh sensitivity

Tests check mass conservation, uniform scalar preservation, finite positive air age, and a Fanger reference point. Comparing 80 × 30 and 120 × 45 meshes changes occupied mean temperature by less than 0.004°C and mean air age by less than 0.2%.

04 · Run it yourself

Clone the linked repository, install requirements, run python -m unittest discover -s tests -v, then python -m iaq. GitHub Actions repeats the tests and analysis. Raw fields, plots, and metrics are included.

04 / Engineering judgment

A useful screen.
A starting point for design.

The screening comparison prioritizes cross-room distribution for further study. The separate 3-D URANS model resolves buoyant momentum and recirculation, but its coarse startup snapshots cannot establish real-room comfort or draft compliance.

  • Potential flow omits momentum transport, turbulence, buoyancy, and jet entrainment.
  • IAQ is represented by air age; CO₂ and contaminant removal are not simulated.
  • Mesh agreement verifies numerical sensitivity, not physical accuracy.
  • Next steps for the executed 3-D model: longer sampling, mesh/timestep refinement, wall-resolution qualification, and experimental validation.

No ISO 7730 or ASHRAE 55 compliance claim is made.
